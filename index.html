For the teacher

Preparation (5 min): print or project the map, picture cards and climate charts. Let groups draw a zone card. Write the 12 words on the board.

Short on time? Only 2–3 groups present. The others give a thumbs up 👍 to the tip they liked best.

Supporting weaker students

  • Give them the Reader or Packing Expert role.
  • Let them use sentence starters and point to pictures.
  • Pre-teach the 4–5 hardest words.
  • Short answers first, then expand: “It is hot.” → “It is hot and dry.”
  • Pair with a stronger partner for rehearsal.
  • A brief word in Russian or Kazakh is OK as a bridge. Then give the English term.

Assessment checklist

Glow and grow: for each group write one thing that was good and one thing to improve. Example: “Glow: accurate temperature description. Grow: use There is for rainfall.” Score content and language separately, so grammar mistakes do not lower the Geography mark.

The 4Cs of CLIL

Geography objective: identify the four climate zones, locate them on a map, describe temperature and rainfall, and compare with Kazakhstan.
English objective: use climate vocabulary and simple present sentences (It is hot. There is a lot of rain.) and give short advice (Take a hat.).
